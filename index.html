Technical Expertise

Power electronics, electrical power systems and the engineering disciplines required to develop and validate high-power conversion equipment.

Power Conversion & Topologies

  • DC-DC and three-phase AC-DC / DC-AC conversion
  • Dual Active Bridge (DAB) and active front-end (AFE) concepts
  • Multilevel converters and modular multilevel converter (MMC) fundamentals
  • SiC MOSFET / IGBT power stages and gate-drive design

Power Systems & Medium-Voltage Fundamentals

  • Three-phase power systems, transformers and electrical machines
  • Grid-connected converters, synchronization and power quality
  • Harmonics, reactive power and power-factor considerations
  • Grounding, insulation coordination and protection fundamentals

Converter Control & Embedded Systems

  • Closed-loop voltage and current control
  • dq-frame control, SRF-PLL and PWM techniques
  • TI C2000 digital controllers and embedded C
  • Controller implementation and hardware integration

Hardware & Magnetics

  • Power-stage schematics, PCB and busbar layout
  • Gate drivers, DC-link design and parasitic inductance
  • Inductors, transformers and thermal design considerations
  • EMI/EMC and high-voltage hardware design principles

Modeling & Engineering Tools

  • MATLAB / Simulink
  • LTspice
  • ANSYS Maxwell
  • KiCad and Altium Designer

Testing, Integration & Delivery

  • Double-pulse testing and switching characterization
  • Converter bring-up, measurement and debugging
  • System integration and experimental validation
  • Engineering planning, documentation and coordination

Medium-voltage power-system topics are presented as relevant engineering fundamentals, not as claims of utility protection-system commissioning or field certification.
